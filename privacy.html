Privacy policy

Session Recorder runs entirely on your own computer. Recordings, transcripts and summaries are stored locally. The app has no server of its own and collects nothing about you.

If you connect Google Drive, the app asks only for permission to create and manage files it creates itself (the drive.file scope). It uploads only the files you choose, to your own Drive. It never reads your other Drive files. You can revoke this access at any time from your Google account's connected apps page.

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
